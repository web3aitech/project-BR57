About

A Cordova roofer, built on follow-through.

Better Roofing 57 LLC is a Cordova-based roofing company with about a decade on homes and businesses across the Memphis metro. We come out the day you call, hand you the estimate before we leave, and check back after the job to make sure nothing is wrong. No runaround, no surprises on the invoice, no empty promises.

Services

Residential & commercial roofing, start to finish.

From a single leaking shingle to a full commercial re-roof, here is what we do.

01

Roof repair

Leaks, missing or damaged shingles, flashing, storm patches. Same-day estimates on most calls.

02

Roof replacement & installation

Asphalt shingle re-roofs for homes and small buildings. Permitting and code-compliant install.

03

Roof inspection

Free inspections for buyers, owners, or after a storm. Written findings you can act on.

04

Commercial roofing

Repairs, replacements, and maintenance for commercial properties across the metro.

05

Storm & insurance help

Document damage, work with your adjuster, and repair to code. Patient with the insurance company.

06

Painting & siding

Exterior painting and siding to match the roof, keeping the rest of the building weather-tight.
