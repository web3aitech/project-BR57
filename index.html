About

A Columbia septic crew that cleans up after itself.

We take crap seriously. Your flush is our rush.

Tennessee Septic and Pumping LLC is based on Hazelwood Rd in Columbia, TN. We pump tanks, install new systems, and repair field lines for homes and businesses across Maury County and the surrounding area. We show up when we say we will, and we leave the yard cleaner than we found it. No runaround, no surprise charges, no skipping the cleanup.

Services

Septic service, start to finish.

From a routine tank pump-out to a brand-new system in the ground, here is what we do.

Septic Tank Pumping

Routine and one-time pump-outs for homes and businesses. We bring the truck, pump the tank, and haul the waste away.

New Septic System Installation

Full new systems, start to finish: excavation, tank set, lines, backfill, and cleanup.

Septic Pump Replacement

Effluent and grinder pumps that have failed or stopped keeping up. We diagnose and replace them.

Septic Field Line Repair

Slow drains, surfacing water, and failing fields. We locate the problem and repair or replace the affected lines.

Septic System Inspection

Inspections for buyers, owners, and real-estate transactions. We open the tank and report what we find.

Emergency Septic Cleaning and Pumping

Backups, overflows, and stopped-up systems. Call and we will get a truck out to you as fast as we can.
